# Brand and Lore

Use this skill for questions about Ghost Orgy's tone, symbolism, the Orchard, or the Nine Sisters.

## Mythology anchors

- Ghost Orgy presents Salt through the Orchard, the Nine Sisters, recovered documents, and damaged ritual audio.
- The Nine Sisters are mortal sinners expelled from Hell, not a conventional band-member roster.
- The Orchard, Watchers, fragments, recovered footage, and artifacts recur throughout the public mythology.

## Source order

1. Start with `/lore/` for the Orchard and its cosmology.
2. Use `/sisters/*.html` for each sister's specific framing.
3. Use `/artifacts/` when the request is about evidence, documentation, or visual traces.
4. Use `/press/` if the answer also needs concise official public language.

## Guardrails

- Do not reduce the Nine Sisters to a literal lineup unless a page explicitly does so.
- Do not invent backstory or production history that the cited pages do not state.
- When the site suggests rather than confirms something, say that it is framed as a motif, trace, or implication.
