GOSOCIAL / connection removal

Data deletion

GOSOCIAL keeps its connection information in the operator's local publishing environment. You can revoke access through Meta, request removal of local connection data, or do both.

Revoke access in Meta

Open the Apps and Websites or Business Integrations section of the Facebook or Instagram account that authorized GOSOCIAL, then remove the connection. Meta will stop future access immediately.

Request local deletion

Email info@unholyghost.org with the subject “GOSOCIAL data deletion request.” Include the Facebook Page name or Instagram handle tied to the connection. We will verify that request against the authorized account, remove the local connection record, and confirm completion.

What is removed

The deletion request removes locally retained connection credentials and publishing receipts associated with the named account. It does not remove content already published on Facebook or Instagram; that content remains under the account owner's control on those platforms.
