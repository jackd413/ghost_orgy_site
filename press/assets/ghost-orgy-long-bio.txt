Ghost Orgy is Jack Dyer's project, built around the Nine Sisters. Each Sister died a sinner and was expelled from Hell when her namesake sin destabilized its structure. They feed an Orchard whose hunger points toward the end of Creation and the return of everything, Orchard included, to the Void. Salt, released May 29, 2026, carries that world through heavy music, damaged texture, sister portraits, recovered records, and ritual audio.

For editors, the closest musical lane is experimental post-hardcore, near Refused, Give Up the Ghost, The Blood Brothers, early mewithoutYou, Modern Life Is War, and The Sound of Animals Fighting. Select licensing, commissions, and collaborations are considered for projects that understand atmosphere as narrative force and can carry the weight of the work.
