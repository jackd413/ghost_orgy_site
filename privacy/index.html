GOSOCIAL / privacy

Privacy

GOSOCIAL is a private, operator-run connection used to publish and manage Ghost Orgy content on authorized Facebook Pages and professional Instagram accounts. It is not a public app or a consumer service.

What the connection accesses

When an authorized administrator connects an account, GOSOCIAL may access the account, Page, Instagram profile, media, and publishing permissions required to create, manage, and verify posts.

How it is used

This access is used only for account administration and content publishing requested by the account owner. GOSOCIAL does not sell account data, use it for advertising, or provide it to third parties.

Where it is kept

Connection credentials and receipts are retained only in the operator's local publishing environment. They are not offered through a public dashboard or shared with other users.

Questions or requests

Email info@unholyghost.org. For removal of connection data, see the data-deletion instructions.
